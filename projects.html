Gamified Psychomotor Vigilance Test (PVT) App

A Unity and C#-based mobile app that delivers research-grade cognitive testing for pediatric and adult users through gamified reaction-time challenges. As a and developer, I built and iterated on 30+ gamified task variants while preserving standardized PVT mechanics, including reaction timing, input handling, and validation logic. Awarded 2nd place at the National Capstone Design Conference Competition and 3rd place at the UCI ICS Project Expo.

Roommate Court

A full-stack web platform that transforms roommate conflicts into a structured "courtroom" workflow. Built dynamic React components for case creation and evidence submission, a jury-based voting system, and an automated AI judge that generates verdicts. Case tracking and historical records are powered by Firebase.

Zot Spot

A hackathon project built to help UC Irvine students quickly locate free period products on campus. An interactive map plots bathroom locations across UCI, with live stock status for pads and tampons, wheelchair accessibility, and distance/walking time, alongside a curated page of menstrual health and wellness resources.

Simple Docs!

This is a version of Google Docs, but was designed for use among young kids, especially for those in elementary school. The app retains a lot of functionality from Google Docs, but has a more appealing and easy-to-use design for children. There are animations and colors everywhere, and no drop-down menus to preserve the convenience and lower the learning curve for the app.
