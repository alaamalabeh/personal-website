Children's Environmental App

This is an app that was specially designed for kids ages 7-14 to find environmental issues that they are passionate about and can post about. I designed the visual aspect of the app, which focuses on a child-friendly design, making it easier for them to use. Simple Docs!

This is a version of Google Docs, but was designed for use among young kids, especially for those in elementary school. The app retains a lot of functionality from Google Docs, but has a more appealing and easy-to-use design for children. There are animations and colors everywhere, and no drop-down menus to preserve the convenience and lower the learning curve for the app.
