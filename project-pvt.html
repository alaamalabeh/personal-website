Gamified Psychomotor Vigilance Test (PVT) App

The Gamified Psychomotor Vigilance Test (PVT) App is a Unity and C#-based mobile application built to deliver research-grade cognitive testing for both pediatric and adult users. As project manager and developer on the capstone team, I helped translate stakeholder research goals into a functional software system built specifically for cognitive testing, balancing scientific rigor with an experience that keeps younger participants engaged.

At its core, the app preserves standardized PVT mechanics — precise reaction timing, input handling, and validation logic — while wrapping them in more than 30 gamified task variants built in 3D. Each variant was designed and iterated on to keep the underlying measurement consistent across very different game skins, so results stay comparable for research purposes no matter which task a participant plays.

Before writing code, I designed low-fidelity and high-fidelity mockups to guide development and user experience, then worked with the team to convert stakeholder research goals into defined system constraints, risks, and plans for future extensibility, so the platform could support long-term research use well beyond the capstone timeline.

The project was recognized with 2nd place at the National Capstone Design Conference Competition and 3rd place at the UCI ICS Project Expo.
