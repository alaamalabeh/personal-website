Children's Environmental App

This app was specially designed for kids ages 7–14 to find environmental issues that they're passionate about and can post about. The goal was to make environmental awareness and civic participation feel approachable for a young audience, rather than abstract or overwhelming.

I designed the visual aspect of the app, focusing on a child-friendly design language — clear icons, bright colors, and simple flows — that makes it easy for younger users to navigate on their own.

The app was mostly created to be used in the classroom and among parents for their kids, so the account and posting flows were designed with parental oversight in mind alongside the kid-facing experience.
