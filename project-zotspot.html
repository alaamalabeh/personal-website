Zot Spot

Zot Spot is a hackathon project (VenusHacks) built to solve a very specific, very real problem: it's often unclear which bathrooms on a college campus actually have period products, or whether they're free. Zot Spot helps UC Irvine students quickly locate free period products across campus instead of guessing.

The centerpiece is an interactive campus map that plots bathroom locations across UCI, each pin showing live stock status for pads and tampons, whether the products are free, wheelchair accessibility, and distance and walking time from the user's location — so someone in need can find the nearest reliable option fast.

Beyond the map, Zot Spot includes a curated resources page linking to campus and community organizations covering menstrual health, wellness, and support services, so the app is useful even beyond the immediate "where is the nearest bathroom" question.

Building this in a hackathon environment meant fast, focused decisions — the priority was getting a genuinely useful tool in front of students rather than polishing every edge case.
