About Me

Hi! I’m Alaa Malabeh, a Software Engineering graduate from the University of California, Irvine, with a passion for building software that is both technically meaningful and genuinely useful.

My experience spans software development, DevOps, research, and user-centered design. During my time at Panasonic Avionics, I worked on CI/CD pipelines, automated testing, and Python-based automation tools that helped streamline engineering workflows. I’ve also developed full-stack applications using technologies such as React, Angular, Node.js, Firebase, and SQL, while exploring how thoughtful UI/UX design can make software more intuitive and accessible.

One of the projects I’m most proud of is my senior capstone project, a gamified Psychomotor Vigilance Test application built with Unity and C#. As the project manager and developer, I helped turn research goals into a functional software system designed for cognitive testing. The project earned 2nd place at the National Capstone Design Conference Competition and 3rd place at the UCI ICS Project Expo.

Beyond development, I’ve been involved in research exploring the impact of AI and large language models on education. I analyzed over 1,200 student survey responses and co-authored a peer-reviewed paper accepted for publication at the International Conference on Computer Science and Education (ICCSE).

I enjoy taking on challenging problems, learning new technologies, and working with teams to turn ideas into real-world solutions. I’m especially interested in opportunities where I can combine software engineering, problem-solving, and creativity to build impactful products.
