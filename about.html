About Me

Hi! I'm Alaa and I'm currently a student at the University of California Irvine (UCI). I am majoring in Software Engineering and minoring in Education Sciences. I grew up in the Los Angeles area and have been studying at UCI for three years now, and am expecting to graduate in June of 2026. My UI/UX interests mostly relate to the intersection of education and programming, and I would love to combine my two interests together to create educational technology. As many of our day to day activities switch to being online, it's important that we work to find new ways to effectively integrate technology in an easy to understand way.
